Lazy Jo’s Universe

Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026

This page explains what happens with your information when you join Lazy Jo’s list or buy music in Lazy Jo’s Universe (lightningwolves.studio/lazyjo).

Who’s responsible

Lazy Jo (Lightning Wolves), Brussels, Belgium. Questions or requests: Lazyjo.official@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Joining the list: your email, your name if you give it, where you signed up, and the exact sentence you agreed to with the date. We use it only to send you news from Lazy Jo (new music, drops, shows), and only after you confirm your email.
  • Buying music: payment happens on Stripe’s secure checkout. We receive your email and what you bought, so we can deliver your download and help if something goes wrong. We never see your card number.
  • Your room (My Room): your records, your first name from the checkout, your supporter number and how you arranged your wall. If you add a photo of yourself to your room, it’s private: only you and Lazy Jo can see it. Your room opens with a private link that only you get. Lazy Jo can look at supporters’ rooms (view only) to see who supports the music; nobody else can.

Who helps us

  • Supabase stores the list (EU region).
  • Resend delivers the emails.
  • Stripe handles payments.
  • Vercel hosts the website.

We don’t sell or share your information with anyone else.

How long we keep it

As long as you’re on the list. When you unsubscribe we stop emailing you straight away; ask us and we’ll delete your details completely. Purchase records are kept as long as the law requires for accounting.

Your rights

You can see, correct or delete your information, and withdraw your consent anytime: use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to Lazyjo.official@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).
